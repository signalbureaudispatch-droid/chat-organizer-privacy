Privacy Policy — Chat Organizer

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Overview

Chat Organizer is a browser extension that helps users organize their AI chat conversations (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, NotebookLM) into folders, search them, and export them.

Data Collection

We collect nothing. Chat Organizer has no servers, no analytics, and no tracking.

  • Chat titles and URLs are scanned from your open browser tabs and stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local.
  • No data is ever sent to any external server by us.
  • Folder assignments and automatic local backups are stored locally only.
  • Exports are generated locally and saved wherever you choose.

Third parties

None. The extension does not communicate with any server other than the AI chat sites you already have open in your browser.

Data removal

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data automatically. You can also export your data to JSON at any time.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at the project repository.
